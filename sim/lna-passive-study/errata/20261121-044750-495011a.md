# Correction to the 1 pF passive study record

The historical V04 used the 1 pF block and ideal shunt. Its original control
gate skipped the reference operating-point rows and RF envelopes. Its row-6
summary used three cells. The following recheck uses the archived measurements
for all five decks at 63 cells each. Inductors are ideal throughout this
comparative study. [Original record](../records/20261121-044750-495011a.md)

| Rechecked gate | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Post-#79 operating points | PASS | All 27 transistor rows at 2.50 V across three HBT processes and three temperatures. Maximum absolute VCE/VBE error was 0.4985 mV. Maximum relative collector-current error was 0.1884% after converting mA to A. |
| Post-#79 RF envelopes | PASS | All 63 V00 cells lie within the published S11, S21, S22, S12, NF and k extrema at all three frequencies at the reference summary’s precision. |
| Passive inertness | PASS | Every passive-extreme control agrees with the typical passive control at the same temperature and supply. |
| Complete, finite measurements | PASS | All 55 method measurements are present and finite at each of the 315 deck/cell combinations. |

Each cell below gives process, temperature (°C) and supply (V). The absolute
minimum is the lowest sweep k measured on that variant. Its paired Δk uses
the control at the same HBT process, temperature and supply. The last two
columns locate the smallest change relative to control anywhere in the grid.

| Historical deck | Minimum k | Limiting cell | Δk there | Minimum Δk | Cell at minimum Δk |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| V01 | 15.2068 | hbt_wcs, −40, 2.75 | +6.2180 | +1.1113 | hbt_wcs, 125, 2.25 |
| V02 | 14.6753 | hbt_wcs, 125, 2.75 | +2.6837 | −0.8511 | hbt_wcs, 125, 2.25 |
| V03 | 1184.6180 | hbt_wcs, −40, 2.75 | +1175.6292 | +1175.6292 | hbt_wcs, −40, 2.75 |
| V04 (1 pF) | 8.7362 | res_bcs/cap_wcs, −40, 2.75 | −0.1448 | −0.4225 | res_bcs/cap_wcs, 27, 2.25 |

The original table’s NF legend has the sign reversed. Differences are variant
minus control. Negative ΔNF means lower noise figure. Negative ΔS21 means
lower gain.

To reproduce the recheck from the repository root, fetch the bundle named
in the historical manifest and pass it to the study verifier. Both commands
verify its SHA-256.

```bash
rid=20261121-044750-495011a
manifest="manifests/artifacts/lna-passive-study-${rid}.manifest.json"
klt artifact get "${manifest}#sim/lna-passive-study/records/${rid}-cells.json.gz" -o "${rid}-cells.json.gz"
python3 sim/lna-passive-study/run.py verify "$rid" --cells "${rid}-cells.json.gz"
```
