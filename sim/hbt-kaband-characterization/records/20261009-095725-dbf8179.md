## Row-3 feasibility screen (device level only)

Target-spec row 3 bounds the **matched LNA's** NF at 50 ohm ports at <= 2.5 dB across the band at all corners. This screen compares that bound with the **bare device's NFmin** (T0 = 290 K) at the best eligible Nx/VCE/J_C per PVT point and frequency (81 PVT point x frequency cells).

- **Worst case**: hbt_wcs_125c_2.25v at 21.2 GHz, NFmin = 1.287 dB, margin `2.5 dB - NFmin` = **+1.213 dB**.
- Best case margin: +2.196 dB. Cells at or above the limit: 0 of 81.
- At T0 = 300.15 K (repo convention) the worst margin is +1.250 dB (hbt_wcs_125c_2.25v at 21.2 GHz).

**Verdict (feasibility screen only)**: the device-level lower bound sits below row 3's 2.5 dB at every PVT point and band frequency, with at least 1.21 dB of headroom at the worst case. That headroom is what a future circuit has to spend on matching-network loss, bias/degeneration noise and the second stage. **It is not evidence that row 3 is achievable**, and nothing here ratifies, relaxes or tests a circuit against it. The margin is also only as good as the device model: it rests on one VBIC card whose 20 GHz noise behaviour was not validated against measurement, with ideal noiseless bias and terminations, so the NFmin values above are optimistic by construction. Zopt is >1 kohm at Nx = 1 and ~170 ohm at Nx = 8, so the matching loss a real circuit must pay is large. Run quality (excluded rows, simulator singular-matrix warnings, self-heating) is stated under Data quality, including how far the reported optima are from the worst of it.

## Limitations

- Bare device, ideal bias tees, ideal noiseless terminations: no matching network, no inductor (none exists in this PDK), no bias-network noise, no interconnect, no pad. A real LNA's NF at row 3's 50 ohm ports adds matching loss (which lands directly on NF) and second-stage noise; Zopt for small Nx is far from 50 ohm (see the per-Nx table), so the matching loss is not small.
- NFmin is grid-sampled in J_C (VBE step 10 mV) and exact in Zs at each sampled bias. The reported J_C optimum is the best sampled point, not an interpolated one.
- Model limits: VBIC Rev.1.15 card; self-heating active (selft=1) and not covered by the ambient validity box; noise-model fidelity at 20 GHz (excess-phase/correlated base-collector noise) is whatever the VBIC card implements -- no measured noise parameters were available to compare against. Deterministic corners only (no mismatch/statistical sections).
- Only Nx in {1, 4, 8} and VCE in {0.6, 1, 1.4} V were run; other values need their own run.
- Gmax: MSG is reported wherever the device is not unconditionally stable (K <= 1 or |Delta| >= 1), which is the case at these frequencies for the bare device -- an MSG is not an achievable stable gain without stabilization.
